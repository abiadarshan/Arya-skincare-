Questions

Before your visit.

Which treatment should I choose?

If you're new to ANYA or unsure what would suit you, The Skin Consultation is a good starting point. We'll talk through your current routine and suggest a suitable studio treatment.

Are your treatments medical?

No. ANYA offers non-medical facial treatments and skincare consultations. We don't diagnose or treat skin conditions. If you have a skin concern, please speak with a qualified medical professional.

Is The Renewal Facial right for everyone?

Not always. Because it focuses on exfoliation, its suitability is assessed before treatment. If it isn't the right fit on the day, we'll suggest a gentler alternative.

How early should I arrive?

Arriving around ten minutes early gives you time to settle in and chat with your therapist before the treatment begins.

Can I change or cancel my appointment?

This website is a prototype, so bookings made here are demonstrations only. In a live studio, changes would usually be handled by message or phone.
